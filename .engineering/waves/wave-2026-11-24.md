# Wave of 2026-11-24: one feature, seven unit stories

This page is the plan of that wave. It stands beside [zero-open-issues.md](zero-open-issues.md) and
follows [wave-2026-11-23.md](wave-2026-11-23.md). Skill: `aep:implementing`, wave mode. The seven
units are `active` (see "Accepted").

## Order

One feature, from three accepted parent stories, source `github:beyond10x/ess#640`: a `via` or an
`instance` reference may name a member of a struct input; synthesis witnesses a row addressed
through it; the generated targets read it. The consumer gap it closes is the first open gap of a
downstream specification.

| parent story | outcome, in the words of its `## Need` |
|---|---|
| `via-and-instance-may-name-a-member-of-a-struct-input` | `via:`, `all:`, `instance:`, `each: {in:}` and `{missing:}` accept an input path into a struct input, a `List<Id>` member included |
| `synthesis-witnesses-a-row-addressed-through-a-struct-input-member` | synthesis arranges and witnesses a row addressed through a struct input member |
| `generated-targets-read-a-reference-nested-in-a-struct-input` | the generated targets read a reference nested in a struct input |

## Decision on the format

Decided, not pending: the feature opens the format `ess/38`. `ess/37`, released in 0.93.0, keeps
its row and its meaning, and nothing in this wave is breaking. s0 opens `ess/38` with no construct
of its own (the newest format, read and compiled exactly as `ess/37`, with the gate the
member-address constructs use below it); s1 and s2 admit their constructs into it, each with a test
that the same text at `ess/37` is refused with its exact message. No suite major moves: the suite
major follows a suite's vocabulary, never the specification format.

Alternatives put and not taken: the new reading inside `ess/37`; refusing the path form of
`missing` at `ess/37`.
