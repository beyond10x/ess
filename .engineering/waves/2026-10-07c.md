## Decisions taken by the coordinator

- #471's cause is synthesis, not the interpreter (`synthesize.rs:13709`, `boundaries` re-sends the
  first identity). It is the same class as #480, so c2 applies c4's synthesis patch
  (`c4-synthesis-boundary-identity.patch`) together with its own; c4 keeps the text diagnostic in

- c1 and c5 both add format rows: c1 owns `crates/edge/ess-xtask/src/docs.rs`,
  `website/docs/reference/formats.md` and `spec-versions.md`; c5 hands its `ess-diff/15` rows as a
  patch, applied by the coordinator at merge.
- #479's "0 authored" part is not a synthesis defect: authored scenarios are selected with
  `--scenarios`, not by `--path` alone (`crates/edge/ess-cli/src/main.rs:609-614`). The issue is
  answered with that when it closes.
- Load was 70 on 20 cores during the release gate; builds wait for the slot and use
  `CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=8` while the load is over 30.
- A gate rerun starts only after the previous gate's process group is gone (`ps -o pid= -g <pgid>` prints nothing). On 2026-10-07 a rerun began while the first loop was still running its later steps, and two gate loops ran in one tree for about an hour.
