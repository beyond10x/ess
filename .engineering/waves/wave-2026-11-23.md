# Wave of 2026-11-23: five stories and twenty written units

This page is the plan of that wave. It stands beside [zero-open-issues.md](zero-open-issues.md),
whose plan is stopped, and follows [wave-2026-11-22.md](wave-2026-11-22.md). Skill:
`aep:implementing`, wave mode. Nothing in this wave is accepted yet (see "Not accepted yet").

## Order

The operator chose five stories on 2026-11-22 (decision D24): the test budget, synthesis on
several threads, writer-side conformance, chosen field values, the mutation audit. Decision D33
of 2026-11-23 replaced the last (see "Change of order"). The five, each with the consumer gap or
rule it serves, in the words of its parent story:

| story | consumer gap or rule |
|---|---|
| `the-test-step-meets-the-test-budget` | the organization rule "Tests stay fast": the test step ends within 2 minutes of wall time and no test runs longer than 60 s |
| `the-caller-readings-of-a-synthesis-run-on-several-threads-and-write-the-suite-a-single-thread-writes` | one synthesis of the largest known downstream specification takes 279 s on one core of a many-core CI runner |
| `a-writer-side-conformance-admits-the-narrowing-a-sender-may-do` | a domain that sends an imported message and always writes its key cannot assert its own copy: the conformance entry is refused, and `reader: true` gives the same refusal |
| `a-setup-run-of-a-creating-branch-that-reads-several-related-rows-creates-its-row-with-the-field-values-another-scenario-chose` | about 26 of an adopter's remaining refusals have this root |
| `an-http-binding-declares-the-largest-request-body-a-route-reads-and-the-refusal-a-larger-one-gets` | a downstream specification's backend answers `413` on 17 routes and its binding cannot say so; the story blocks an accepted story of a consumer |

## Change of order

Decision D33 (2026-11-23, taken by the operator's delegate under the operator's delegation)
replaced the mutation audit of pick D24 by the request-body cap story. Reason: the cap story
blocks an accepted story of a consumer, and the organization rule "Goal first" orders waves by
the consumer's first gap. The mutation audit is not dropped: its parent story
`a-mutation-audit-of-a-larger-model-ends-in-bounded-time-and-shows-its-progress-while-it-runs`
stays `active`, its two units (`m1`, texts written; `m2`, texts written) are not created in this
store and wait for the wave after this one. `m1` prints progress on standard error; `m2` is a
design page for reusing suites across mutants.

  last (its ceilings are measured on the tree that holds the others). `slow_lane.rs`,

  later unit keeps both sides. `story:one-integration-test-binary-per-large-crate` lands after
  all seven units, not between them: markers and exemptions name today's binaries.
- Threads: p1 and p2 share `made.rs` and `witness_memo.rs` in different functions; either lands
  first. p4 shares `main.rs`, `cli.md` and `CHANGELOG.md` with the mutation audit's `m1`, which is
  not in this wave, so no conflict arises here. p5 shares `Taskfile.yml` with t1 (t1 first) and
  the xtask `main.rs` with any test-budget unit that adds a subcommand.
- Writer: strictly serial, w1 to w2 to w3. w1 and w2 share `references` in `conformance.rs` and
  the test directory `writer_conformance`; w1 creates it. Only w3 edits `CHANGELOG.md`. w1 opens
  `ess-composition/6`; two later stories (`reader-true-refused-for-closed-readers`, and
  `a-synthesized-suite-sends-an-omitted-or-null-member-both-ways`) reuse it and add only their
  own key.
- Chosen values: c1 and the correction unit both rewrite `drive_several` and `as_own_scenario`
  in `related_guard.rs`; c1 starts after the correction is merged. `CHANGELOG.md` is shared with

- Request body cap: h1 first (binding keys, IR, `ESS-HTTP-034`, accessors, one arm in the
  OpenAPI projection, design page keys and codes, `formats.md`); h2, h3 and h4 need only h1 and
  run at the same time. `Taskfile.yml`, `AGENTS.md` and one module line of the Rust server test
  file are shared by h2, then h3 (adjacent lines in the same lists). The OpenAPI projection's
  source file is shared by h1 (one arm), then h4. The design page is shared by h1 (keys, codes,
  refusals, refinement) and h4 (precedence, servers, OpenAPI) with no common line. `CHANGELOG.md`
  is h4's only, `formats.md` h1's only; h4 shares no file with h2 or h3. The units add no new
  format version.

## Decisions already taken for this wave

- Threads (decision D24, 2026-11-22, option A, on the parent's `## Decisions taken`): D1 A the
  default is one thread; D2 C stage 2 on two threads, one per uniform model; D3 A caller readings
  only; D4 A the flag is `--jobs`. A measurement of peak memory at 2 and 4 threads comes before
  any default changes.
- Test budget (parent `## Decisions`, 2026-11-21): D1 option b, generated outputs committed with
  a digest of their sources and compiled once per run in the slow lane; D2 option c, the archives
  shrink through `one-integration-test-binary-per-large-crate`, after the seven units; D3 option
  b, the feature-off run moves to the slow lane (its own units are not in this cut).
- Request body cap (D33, on the story's `## Decisions taken`): the story's own recommendations
  stand. D1 `limits.request_bytes` at binding and route level; D2 `refusals.too_large` as a
  fourth closed key; D3 integer bytes; D4 a ceiling of 16 MiB on a declared cap; D5 the fixed
  1 MiB stays the default; D6 the check at head time with the matched route's cap; D7 no `413` in
  OpenAPI for a binding that declares nothing. The requester confirmed that a binding default
  with a per-route override and a declarable refusal body fit its routes.
- Writer (the units' own `## Decisions`, taken by their authors under the parent's
  recommendations): `writer: true` mirrors `reader: true`; both keys on one entry are refused;
  an imported field with no presence is read as the published schema reads it; the full dual of
  the reader widenings is built; the format number is shared with the other stories that open
  `ess-composition/6`.
- Choices made by the authors that the plan critics may veto: c1 takes option B of the parent's
  D1 (the chosen values enter as fixed input fields), against the parent's recommendation of A,
  because `selects` takes one row; c1 leaves the existence family's `drive_toward` as it is; the
  threads unit p3 builds the recommended option A, each uniform model's readings on one thread
  (about 2.2 times, inferred, against the parent's 3.5 times); w3 gates `ess-cli` on its binary's
  tests and Clippy only; an imported bare `Json` admits nothing under `writer`.

## Not accepted yet

The units are drafts. The plan critics (`aep:plan-critic-acceptance`, `-design`, `-scope`,
`-parallel-safety`) read them before any is accepted; a unit moves to `proposed` and `active`
only after that. The request body cap story is `active` under D33; its units are drafts too. The
units table above is the order the authors propose, not an approved wave.

## Open

What the authors could not verify by reading:

- Test budget: whether cargo accepts `--test` for a target held by one of several `-p` packages
  (t1 loops per package); the wall time of `task test-slow` and the per-test CI times (t7 uploads
  JUnit artifacts); the nextest `test(/regex/)` dialect at the pinned version; `timeout
  --kill-after` around the pool's cargo shim; the cause of the over-budget targets (every unit
  starts with a measurement and a decision rule). One choice for the operator, in t4: mark the
  nine compile tests slow and keep their in-test cargo builds (recommended), or port them to
  Taskfile harness tasks (a larger follow-up).
- Threads: debug-build test times; that `Synthesis` is `Send` (believed yes); whether the
  component-scope test in p3 goes red when the carried context is removed; the real speed-up and
  the memory at 2 and 4 jobs, which p5 measures.
- Writer: whether the inline test models validate; the exact YAML of enum attributes, unions and
  commands; the exact text printed for a declared-type drift; whether `task test-xtask` pins a
  count in the format catalogue page.
- Chosen values: whether c1's tests go green and which pin rows move; what `drive_several`
  receives for a creation with a chosen input; whether the created row's recorded values match
  what is sent; the correction's final memo key shape; the debug-build times of the new tests.
- Request body cap: the generated Go entry point's real directory name, and whether the fixture
  model has unmet obligations in Go; the model digest of the fixture under its source name
  (captured by the implementor); that `-D warnings` on the generated Rust crate is clean;
  whether a Go refusal on a full-body send of more than 256 KiB can reset the client's
  connection (the tests send heads only above that size); the wall time of `task test-xtask`
  and `ci_lanes` against the 120 s step; `task site-build` (network, left to CI); the line
  numbers of the design page's precedence list after h1's edits shift them (the units say to
  find by heading); that `http::TOO_LARGE` fits the status-constant convention beside
  `UNFINISHED`. Two corrections to the parent text: `diagnostics.md` holds no `ESS-HTTP` rows,
  so no unit edits it, and a non-integer limit is a parse error of the typed field, so
  `ESS-HTTP-034` covers the range and bodiless routes only.
- Follow-ups outside this cut: the feature-off move to the slow lane; one story per crate for
  the 172 test files that spawn node, tsc, go, cargo, rustc or npm in the step; a matrix split of
  the slow job if `task test-slow` runs over 20 minutes; a story in the slot repository naming
  the exceptions found by t6; raising the default job count after p5's table; the mutation
  audit's two units.
