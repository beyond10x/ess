# Wave of 2026-11-23: five stories and twenty-one unit stories

This page is the plan of that wave. It stands beside [zero-open-issues.md](zero-open-issues.md),
whose plan is stopped, and follows [wave-2026-11-22.md](wave-2026-11-22.md). Skill:
`aep:implementing`, wave mode. The 21 units were accepted on 2026-11-23 (see "Accepted on
2026-11-23").

## Order

The operator chose five stories on 2026-11-22 (decision D24): the test budget, synthesis on
several threads, writer-side conformance, chosen field values, the mutation audit. Decision D33
of 2026-11-23, taken by the operator's delegate under the operator's delegation, replaced the
last by the request-body cap: the cap story blocks an accepted story of a consumer, and the
organization rule "Goal first" orders waves by the consumer's first gap. The mutation audit is
not dropped: its parent story
`a-mutation-audit-of-a-larger-model-ends-in-bounded-time-and-shows-its-progress-while-it-runs`
stays `active`, and its two units (`m1`, a progress line on standard error; `m2`, a design page on
reusing suites across mutants) have their texts written and wait for the wave after this one.

The five, each with the consumer gap or rule it serves, in the words of its parent story:

| story | consumer gap or rule |
|---|---|
| `the-test-step-meets-the-test-budget` | the organization rule "Tests stay fast"; this wave's outcome: a slow lane exists, six kinds of expensive test leave the test step, a test over 60 s is killed, the step ceilings are pinned (the 2-minute end state is not reached by this wave, see "Follow-ups") |
| `the-caller-readings-of-a-synthesis-run-on-several-threads-and-write-the-suite-a-single-thread-writes` | one synthesis of the largest known downstream specification takes 279 s on one core of a many-core CI runner |

### Shared files and who lands first

- Byte-pin stamp: the stamp unit (t2) is the only unit that writes the stamp file, and it lands
  after every unit that changes a source its stamp hashes: the threads unit p3, the
  chosen-values unit c1, and the request-body units h1 and h4. `ess-synth` is not hashed, so h2
  and h3 are not predecessors. No other unit's Gate runs t2's digest test. A unit that lands after
  t2 and changes a hashed source re-stamps in its own change; none is planned.
- Model tables: only the chosen-values unit c1 re-pins the three model tables, with the commands
  they print today, before t2 exists. p1, p2 and p3 run the table targets and re-pin nothing.
  The second of p1 and c1 to land reruns the three table targets on the merged tree before it
  lands; a row that differs is c1's re-pin, regenerated with the table's own command, never
  merged by hand.
- Slow lane: t1a (the local mechanism) first; t2 to t5 and p3 depend on it. t1b (the CI wiring)
  needs only t1a and is built beside t3 to t5. Slow-lane table: t2, t3, t4, t5 and the threads
  unit p3 each add a row to `MARKED` in `crates/edge/ess-xtask/tests/slow_lane.rs` (p3's row is
  the corpus test `every_repository_model_and_component_writes_its_bytes_at_one_two_and_seven_jobs`);
  keep every row at merge. p3's Gate runs `cargo test -p ess-xtask --test slow_lane`; the slow
  profile kills a test at 20 minutes, the 60 s bound does not apply there.
- Allows: every `allow(dead_code)` p2 leaves in `ess-conformance/src` carries the comment
  `p3 removes this`; p3 removes each line with that comment, whichever file holds it, and nothing
  else there.
- `ci_lanes.rs`: t1b, t3, t6, t7 and h3 each append their own functions; keep both sides at merge.
- `Taskfile.yml` and `AGENTS.md`: h2, then h3, then t7 (t7 adds `timeout` prefixes to the `test`,
  `test-packages` and `test-shard` tasks that h2 and h3 edit); p5 after t1a on `Taskfile.yml`.
- `crates/edge/ess-cli/src/main.rs`, `website/docs/reference/cli.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`: w3 before
  p4; the second regenerates `cli.md` with the generator, never merges it by hand; every unit that
  adds a changelog bullet keeps both bullets at merge.
- Threads: p1, then p2 (after p1 and c1; c1's counter `OWN_OVERLAY_WORK` is classified by p2),
  then p3 (after p1, p2 and t1a), then p4 and p5. p1 and p2 share `made.rs` and `witness_memo.rs`
  in different functions.
- Writer: strictly serial, w1 to w2 to w3. w1 opens `ess-composition/6` and creates the test
  directory `writer_conformance`; w2 reuses w1's presence rule (`absent_read`) and adds none of
  its own. Only w3 edits `CHANGELOG.md` among the three.
- Chosen values: c1 and the correction unit of the wave of 2026-11-22 both rewrite `drive_several`

  left to CI).
