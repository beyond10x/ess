## Intent

Release ESS 0.61.0 with wave k: stage 2 of the `ess-http/1` binding (request encoding, response body and status per outcome, response headers, challenges and redirects, the OpenAPI projection, and the synthesized Rust and Go servers), which closes https://github.com/beyond10x/ess/issues/493, and a component that plays a component of a pinned contract specification (`ess-composition/4` `roles:`), which closes https://github.com/beyond10x/ess/issues/525.
