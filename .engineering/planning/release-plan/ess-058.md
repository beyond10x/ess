adopter's priority order. Each was assessed with `.agents/skills/assessing-external-requests` before
its artifact was written; minimal reproductions are under `.engineering/repro/<issue>/`.

## Decided

#498 and #500 need source format `ess/24`; they moved to `release-plan:ess-24-one-language` (`decision-blocker:ess-058-format-24`, cleared). 0.58.0 ships #496, #499 and #501; #497 is answered and closed. #499 goes first, because it needs no held file; #496 and #501 follow once the other session's synthesis wave is done with `synthesize.rs` and `synthesize/`.
