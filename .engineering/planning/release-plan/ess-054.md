## Intent

Release ESS 0.54.0 carrying a resolution for every beyond10x/ess issue filed on 2026-10-05:
#426–#430 and #432–#459 (33 issues; #431 is the 0.53.0 bundle pull request, not an issue in scope).
Operator instruction, 2026-10-05: reconcile only that day's issues, plan them with the AEP planning
store, dispatch them in several waves, and ship them together as 0.54.0.

## Base

0.54.0 stacks on 0.53.0. The integration branch `integrate/ess-054` forks
`batch/ui-live-apps-complete-20261003` at `087935463f` (the head of PR #431). The 0.53.0 coordinator
confirmed on 2026-10-05 that the branch will not be rewritten and that 0.53.0 lands as a bot merge
commit of PR #431, tagged `0.53.0`. When that lands, `main` is merged into `integrate/ess-054`.

## Reconciliation (2026-10-05)

- No issue of the day duplicates an open older issue; every older open issue is claimed by PR #431.
- The 0.53.0 changelog resolves none of them; several extend a 0.53.0 construct (#438 on #200,
  #439 on #244, #440 on #268/#285, #442 on #299, #428 on #360).
- One story per issue, `story:feature-request-<N>`, tagged `ess-0.54.0`, each with a Fit review and
  a Decisions section per `.agents/skills/assessing-external-requests/SKILL.md`. #426 is split into
  `feature-request-426a` and `feature-request-426b`; the generator itself belongs to Canon.
- `story:ess-generate-check` is split out of #435 by coordinator decision. It ships a new public flag,
  `ess generate --check`, matching the `--check` its siblings `generate cli`, `schema typescript`,
  `realization generate` and `ui docs` already have, so the #435 guide can show adopters a drift
  check they can run. #435 depends on it.

#458, #459, and #442 if it needs one). One suite-format pair, `ess-conformance/44` and `/45`, shared by
every 0.54.0 suite change (#427, and #438 if list-valued view parameters need it). The story that
lands first introduces the version; later ones extend it.
