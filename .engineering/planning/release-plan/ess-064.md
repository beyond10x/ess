## Intent

Release ESS 0.64.0 with wave n: no published crate depends on `serde_yaml` or `unsafe-libyaml`. This closes https://github.com/beyond10x/ess/issues/541, so consumers can drop their RUSTSEC-2024-0449 ignore. Wave n runs after wave m and does not go ahead of 0.60.0 or waves k to m.
