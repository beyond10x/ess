## Decided in the operator's absence

- **The set.** Every non-terminal story decomposing the epic moves to `active`, after two rounds of
  the plan-critic panel (`review-result:zero-issues-*-round-1`, `-round-2`). Round 2, the last the procedure allows, still asked for revision; every finding of
  both rounds was answered before the moves and carries a `review_outcome` on its artifact.
- **One format per kind for the whole epic.** Every new source spelling lands in `ess/37`, every new
  suite shape in `ess-conformance/64` (ordinary) and `/65` (coverage), and every new diff change
  kind in `ess-diff/24`, all three opened empty by
  `story:ess-37-and-conformance-64-65-are-open-for-the-epics-constructs`; the epic's other format
  moves are named by the stories that take them (`ess-scenario/7`,
  `ess-composition/6`, the mutation manifest and report). `docs/design/generic-language-features.md`
  § policy 2 asks for fewer formats, and one release carries them all.
- **Shared surfaces** (epic § Shared surfaces) are reconciled by the coordinator at merge, not
  typed scope.
- **#735**: an exact author-declared scenario id; the occurrence is kept and marked covered.
  **#757**: an exchange with `follows: {view:}`. Both are the triage agents' recommended answers.
- **Choices the revision made where an acceptance let two results pass**, each recorded in its
  story's `## Decisions`; the ones that change shipped behaviour:
  `story:the-interpreter-and-synthesis-answer-a-guarded-move-before-not-found-alike` answers a fresh
  identity `not-found` (design § 2a, G4), which retires part of an implemented story's
  `ESS-SYNTH-003` refusal; `story:generated-rust-and-go-readers-agree-on-leading-zeros` makes the
  unbound Go server read `01` as the Rust server does; `story:generated-decoders-enforce-declared-value-constraints`
  makes `--target typescript` refuse a type with a published constraint by name;

  plain `Optional` in the generated Rust server; `story:served-committed-command-answers-its-outcome`
  removes `Refused::Undelivered`.
- **Scope.** Six self-declared gaps citing no open issue left the epic; two stories were narrowed to
