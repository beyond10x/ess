# UXF-010: reference-table rows many screens down

`ess-31-release` (find the release that introduced `ess/31`) takes 26 actions at desktop-1280 and
35 at mobile-390 against an expectation of 6; 24 and 31 of them scroll the format version table.
On `/ess/docs/reference/spec-versions` (60,406 px tall at 1280 px) each row carries a full
description: the `ess/31` row alone is 1,459 px tall and the table 19,705 px. The table of
contents names the families, not the versions, so it does not help.

`diagnostic-type-009` shows the same, smaller: 6 and 7 actions against 5, with 4 scroll gestures
on `/ess/docs/reference/diagnostics`.

No search (UXF-001) means scrolling is the only way to a row.

## R-02: held to the card

R-02 holds every pointer run to its card's `max_actions` (gate 4) with a screen-only user that
scans the page from where it is, down to the end and back up, instead of scrolling straight to a
row it could not see (review of R-01, T6 to T9). `ess-31-release` takes 25 actions at desktop-1280
and 33 at mobile-390 against 6; `diagnostic-type-009` takes 7 at mobile-390 against 5 (4 at
desktop-1280). The asset is the card's L3 test, which fails while the run is over the limit.
