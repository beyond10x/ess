# UXF-030: the model of the visualise page exists only inside the frame

`website/docs/visualise.md` holds a lead paragraph and the frame. A reader who cannot use the frame
(a keyboard or screen-reader user facing its serious nodes, a phone reader) has the model as text
nowhere on the page. None of the 22 task runs reaches the page, so no round metric sees it.

UXF-005 to UXF-008 are the templates package's and wait on its fix; this is the part the site owns.
A decision that names the pin as owner of the frame's nodes belongs with them, not a hidden page.

Affected: every visit to `/ess/docs/visualise` and to the landing page's graph; 2 of 139 pages.
Cost M.

One line: write the model as a text table on the page from the presentation file, by the same
command that writes the file, and hold its entity count with an L1 test.
