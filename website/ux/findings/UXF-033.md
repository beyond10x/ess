# UXF-033: the bot's click point is the centre of the link's bounding box

For a link wrapped over two lines the centre of its box can lie in the gap between the lines or on
text that is not the link, so the click misses. In R-06 the status page was edited so that its
link starts its line, which fits the site to the bot. The tool should click a point inside the
link's first line box (its first client rect).

Affected: any task whose link wraps, on a phone most of all. Cost S.

One line: click inside the first client rect of the link, and prove it on a wrapped link.
