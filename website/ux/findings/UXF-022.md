# UXF-022: the sidebar out of view in the gated states on two short pages

Gate 2 gates the docs and blog sidebars at the top and the middle of a page (D15,
`rounds/R-02/decision.md`). On two pages it fails there at desktop-1280, in both themes:

| page | element | configurations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/ess/docs/getting-started` | docs sidebar: out of viewport | desktop-1280 light, dark |
| `/ess/releases/tags` | blog sidebar: out of viewport | desktop-1280 light, dark |

The same two pages fail at the measured-only sizes 1440 and 1024 in both themes (`round.json`
`measured_only`). UXF-002 and UXF-003 are accepted under D15 and cover the `end` state only, so
these failures had no record.

The every-merge gate misses them: neither page is a template page (`adapter.ts`,
`TEMPLATE_PAGES`), and the gate runs `gate-7` to `gate-9` pass gate 2 on 9 of 9 pages. The slow
lane's L2 test of each page is red on it. `round.json` holds a count per element, not the state,
so whether the failure is at the top or the middle is not recorded; the cause (a sidebar taller
than the page's scroll range, or the sticky column) is inferred.

Affected: 2 of 139 pages, 4 of 278 desktop configurations. Cost S.

One line: add a short doc page and a release tag page to `TEMPLATE_PAGES`, and have the reporter
name the page and state of each gate 2 failure.
