# UXF-009: terminal titles cut at 390 px

The terminal sessions of the landing page and the visualise page title their bar
(`span.b10x-terminal__title`). At mobile-390 two titles are cut with an ellipsis, and neither a
`title` nor an `aria-label` carries the full text: 6 texts on 2 pages (`/ess/`, `/ess/docs/visualise`),
both themes.

## R-05: closed by one style rule

`website/src/css/custom.css` lets a terminal title wrap at any width (`.b10x-terminal__bar >
.b10x-terminal__title`: no ellipsis, normal white space). A rule on the title alone lost to the
shared theme's rule of the same weight, which comes later in the stylesheet. Before (`gate-4` in R-05's `round.json`): 2
truncated texts at `/ess/` and 1 at `/ess/docs/visualise`, mobile-390, each theme, 6 in all. After
(R-05's `round.json`): 0. Its assets stay in the gate, now as assets of UXF-005 and UXF-008, which fail on
other messages; a truncated title fails them as a new failure.
