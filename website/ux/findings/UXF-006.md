# UXF-006: text below the contrast minimum

axe `color-contrast` (WCAG 1.4.3, serious): 22 nodes on 2 pages, 8 configurations, in both themes
and at both gating viewports: `/ess/docs/visualise` and `/ess/releases/archive`. The shot outlines
the first node on the archive.

## Owner (R-05)

The visualise page renders `<EssPresentation>` from `@beyond10x/ess-ui-templates`, pinned at `655a7e58bc66800c7b891515af2dc16c3a572782` in `website/package.json` (a commit of its branch `docs/serves-section`, pull request 4). Its `main` (`5b77e77`, tag 0.1.0) changed nothing under `web/` since that commit, so no newer commit of the package fixes the frame. The frame is the package's: this site does not copy or override its components, and the record waits on a fix there.

The release archive's part is this site's: its title sits in a `hero--primary` band, whose text colour the stock theme sets to `--ifm-font-color-base-inverse` on the accent background.

## R-05: the release archive passes

The archive's `h1` took `--ifm-heading-color` (the theme's heading colour, `--b10x-color-heading`)
on the accent band. `website/src/css/custom.css` gives a `hero--primary` band and its heading the
theme's on-accent colour (`--b10x-color-on-accent`). Before (`gate-4` in R-05's `round.json`): 1 `color-contrast` node in each
of the archive's 4 configurations. After (R-05's `round.json`): none, and both archive tests pass, so they are
no longer assets of this record. The visualise page's 8 and 1 nodes stay with the templates package
(Owner, above).
