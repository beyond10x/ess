# UXF-025: the gate step over 120 s under load

The organization's test budget ends a repository's every-merge test step within 120 s. R-03's gate
lane ran 62 tests in `gate-4`: 36 L1, 19 L2, 7 L3 (`../rounds/R-03/round.json`; corrected in R-05 from 63
and 8 L3). Its three runs in `round.json` took 119.6 s (cut by the
gate's `globalTimeout`, status `timedout`), 100.7 s and 93.6 s; three earlier runs were cut at
118 to 119 s, and one run without the cut took 220.7 s (`report.md`, notes).

Where the time went (report review, § 3 (a), from the kept gate logs):

- `/ess/docs/reference/spec-versions` and `/ess/docs/reference/diagnostics` hold 100 to 133 of the
  321 to 400 test-seconds of the gate (24 to 32 s per test in `gate-9`).
- The three nested Playwright runs of `specs/l1-findings.spec.ts` take 12 to 14 s each; with the
  frame fixture they raised the L1 sum from 11.5 to 14 test-seconds (R-02 engine) to 38 to 62.
- The tool's own share of the rise is 9 to 15 s of wall time; the rest swings with the host's load.

This record supersedes UXF-014, closed in R-02 at 28.7 to 41.6 s per gate step, which names the
same budget; R-03's frame repair (UXF-021) and the known-failure tests raised the step again.

Affected: 1 of the 3 gate runs `round.json` holds was cut. Cost M.

One line: move the two heavy table pages and the three nested runs to the slow lane, keep a lighter
page of each kind in the gate, and prove three runs under 120 s at the host's load.

## R-06: the diagnosis, and where the budget is judged

`../rounds/R-05/diagnosis-gate-time.md` read the kept timing of 20 gate runs of R-03 to R-05
(read-only; every number below is its own, measured unless it says inferred):

- Wall time is 0.31 × the sum of test times (the mean of wall / S over 12 complete runs: 0.284 to
  0.365, at a 1-minute load of 23 to 131). Load does not enter that ratio; it enters the test times.
- Host load dilates each test: browser tests took 2.0 s each at load 23, 3.3 s at 61 and 19.9 to
  22.4 s at 95 to 150; the visualise page's L2 test 3.6 s at load 23 and 33 to 45 s at 95 to 150,
  up to 14 times. The shape is a knee between load 61 and 95, not a line.
- Every cut run ended at the global cap (`Timed out waiting 120s for the test suite to run`), with
  0 new failures; no test met its own 60 s limit (longest 51.2 s on R-05's final engine). All 8
  runs that started at a load of 115 or more were cut; every pass started at 73 or less.
- The four known-failing browser tests (`L2 /ess/docs/visualise` and `L2 /ess/` at both gating
  viewports) hold 31 to 36 % of the test-seconds at load 133 to 170 (`gate-5`, `gate-7`).
- Outside the gate's own clock: the pool's queue (12 to 532 s in the 5 runs that logged it) and
  server start and `npm ci` (in no kept number).

The host's working rule since 2026-11-23: a test budget (60 s per test, 120 s per step) is
judged in CI and locally at a 1-minute load of 60 or less (3 times the 20 cores); a local step
prints the load beside its time; a run over budget at a higher load is reported, not counted as a
defect. By that rule `gate-8` (load 60.06, 19.5 s) is the only R-05 gate run that judges the
budget, and it holds it; `gate-2` to `gate-7` are reported.

## R-06: the probe, and the two page kinds the gate gives up

The probe of the diagnosis ran first, on the engine of the R-06 triage
(`../rounds/R-06/probe.json`): three interleaved pairs, A the gate lane, B the same lane without
the four known-failing browser tests. B took 0.81, 1.23 and 0.31 of A's wall time; the refutation
needed more than 0.85 in two pairs of three, or a B run over 110 s at a load of 130 to 150, and
neither happened. The pairs did not run at equal load (pair 2's B started at 108.35, its A at
81.5), and every run started above 60, so the probe is taken above load 60.

Change 1 of R-06 (decision `R-06-gate-kinds` in `../rounds/R-06/decision.md`): the gate lane
runs the template pages of every page kind but `landing` and `doc-without-sidebar`, whose four L2
tests run in the slow lane only, and `ux-gate` prints the 1-minute load before and after beside
its wall time; a step cut at its budget says whether the load was above 60. The finding stays
open until gate runs at a load of 60 or less hold the budget; their values are in this round's
`round.json` and its report.

## R-06: closed

The three gate runs of R-06's measurement, on the final engine, each started at a 1-minute load
under 60 and ended well inside the 120 s budget with no new failure; their wall times and loads are
in the round-values block below and in `../rounds/R-06/round.json`. By the host's working rule the

browser-test count, and the gate lane's `globalTimeout` of 120 s, which still ends and fails a
