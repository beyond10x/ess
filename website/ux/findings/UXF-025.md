# UXF-025: the gate step over 120 s under load

The organization's test budget ends a repository's every-merge test step within 120 s. R-03's gate
lane ran 63 tests: 36 L1, 19 L2, 8 L3. Its three runs in `round.json` took 119.6 s (cut by the
gate's `globalTimeout`, status `timedout`), 100.7 s and 93.6 s; three earlier runs were cut at
118 to 119 s, and one run without the cut took 220.7 s (`report.md`, notes).

Where the time went (report review, § 3 (a), from the kept gate logs):

- `/ess/docs/reference/spec-versions` and `/ess/docs/reference/diagnostics` hold 100 to 133 of the
  321 to 400 test-seconds of the gate (24 to 32 s per test in `gate-9`).
- The three nested Playwright runs of `specs/l1-findings.spec.ts` take 12 to 14 s each; with the
  frame fixture they raised the L1 sum from 11.5 to 14 test-seconds (R-02 engine) to 38 to 62.
- The tool's own share of the rise is 9 to 15 s of wall time; the rest swings with the host's load.

This record supersedes UXF-014, closed in R-02 at 28.7 to 41.6 s per gate step, which names the
same budget; R-03's frame repair (UXF-021) and the known-failure tests raised the step again.

Affected: 1 of the 3 gate runs `round.json` holds was cut. Cost M.

One line: move the two heavy table pages and the three nested runs to the slow lane, keep a lighter
page of each kind in the gate, and prove three runs under 120 s at the host's load.

## R-04: repaired in the tool, open until a run at load 90

- The gate's template pages are at most 1,200 built elements (`GATE_PAGE_ELEMENTS` in
  `adapter.ts`): the format history (3,405) and the diagnostics page (1,897) run in the slow lane;
  the lowering reference (1,049 elements), a generated reference table page too, takes their place.
- The three nested Playwright runs moved to `specs/nested-findings.spec.ts`, the slow lane's
  `nested` project.
- The gate runs its longest tests first (projects `l3`, `l2`, `l1`; keyboard and phone runs first;
  the template pages slowest first), so the four workers end together.
- The asset, an L1 test, failed before (2 template pages over the limit, `l1-findings.spec.ts`
  starting a nested run) and passes after.

Three timed runs of the gate on this change, 4 workers, the 1-minute load logged before and after
each: 45.1 s (53.48, 50.19), 28.5 s (50.19, 45.09), 30.1 s (45.09, 41.25). R-03's three runs took
93.6 to 119.6 s at a logged load of 61 to 69. The host stayed under load 60, so load 90 is a
projection: the runs held 97.6 to 149.9 test-seconds, and their wall time was 0.29 to 0.31 of
that. Scaled by the load (90 / 50 = 1.8) the slowest run takes about 81 s; scaled by the largest
spread seen between two runs at the same logged load (2.9 in the L2 test-seconds of `gate-2` and
`gate-3` of R-04's first runs, the second beside a Rust compile) it takes about 130 s. So the step is not shown to end inside 120 s at load 90, and the record
stays open until a gate run at that load does.
