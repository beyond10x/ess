# UXF-021: gate 5 lost three rules on an unchanged site

R-01 `gates.gate5_accessibility.by_rule` held 5 rules, 2,733 nodes on 104 pages:
`nested-interactive` 2 nodes, `scrollable-region-focusable` 1, `color-contrast` 22 nodes on 2
pages, `label-content-name-mismatch` 6 on 2 pages, `link-in-text-block`. R-02 holds 3 rules,
2,732 nodes on 105 pages: `color-contrast` 4 nodes on 1 page (`/ess/releases/archive`, the `h1`),
`label-content-name-mismatch` 4 on 1 page (`/ess/`), `link-in-text-block` 2,724 on 103 pages. No
`nested-interactive`, no `scrollable-region-focusable`, 18 fewer `color-contrast` nodes.

The site did not change between the rounds; `core/contract.ts` did: R-02 runs axe with
`setLegacyMode(true)`. The report reviewer infers that mode as the cause and did not prove it.

UXF-007 and UXF-008 are open and no longer visible in the numbers, and the R-02 baseline
(`baseline.json`) took the smaller set as an improvement. A round that restores the rules will read
as worse against it: that rise is the repaired measurement and needs its own decision.

Affected: gate 5 on 2 of 139 pages (`/ess/docs/visualise`, `/ess/releases/archive`); the baseline
of all 556 page configurations. Cost M.

One line: find the cause on the two pages UXF-007 and UXF-008 name, and hold gate 5 to every rule
with an L1 test on a doctored page.
