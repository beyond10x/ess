# UXF-027: the index lists cannot be skipped and a jump lands unmarked

Round R-04 (UXF-010) put an index under the title of `/ess/docs/reference/spec-versions` (37 links,
`ess/1` to `ess/37`) and of `/ess/docs/reference/diagnostics` (19 links, `001` to `019`). Each is
one paragraph of links separated by " · ".

- Keyboard: 37 and 19 tab stops in a row, with no heading, no landmark and no label; a
  screen-reader user cannot skip the list or learn what it is before it starts.
- Words: the diagnostics list offers bare numbers; a reader who holds `UNSUPPORTED` does not know
  it is `009`.
- Scope: the versions list covers the `ess/` family only, and does not say so.
- Landing: the jump target is an empty `<a id>` in a table cell; the site's stylesheet gives it no
  scroll margin and no `:target` style, so the row may land under the sticky navbar and nothing
  marks it among rows up to 1,459 px tall.

Affected: the two task cards that pass a landing, `ess-31-release` and `diagnostic-type-009`
(3 of 22 task runs in R-04). Cost S.

One line: put each list in a `nav` with an accessible name, name the class beside its number,
say which family the versions list covers, and give a jump target a scroll margin and a
`:target` mark.
