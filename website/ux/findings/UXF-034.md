# UXF-034: canned reporter lines in the real gate log

One L1 test drives the reporter with canned tests, and the canned reporter prints its "ux: NEW"
lines into the log of every gate run. A reader of a gate log, or a script that greps it for new
failures, sees failures that are not the run's.

Affected: every gate log. Cost S.

One line: capture the canned reporter's output in the test, and assert that none of it reaches the
log.
