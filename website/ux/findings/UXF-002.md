# UXF-002: the docs sidebar scrolls up under the navbar at the end of a doc page

At desktop-1280, scrolled to the end of a doc page, the sidebar column ends where the footer
starts. The sticky sidebar inside it is pushed up, and its first categories sit under the navbar
(the shot: "Start here" and "Concepts" are gone, "Guides" is cut).

67 doc pages, 134 configurations (both themes), 136 failures, all in the `end` scroll state.
Examples: `/ess/docs`, `/ess/docs/concepts/adoption-modes`, `/ess/docs/concepts/component-delivery`.
The measured-only desktop-1440 and desktop-1024 have an always-visible element out of view on 12
of the 13 pages measured there (this sidebar or the release-post one, UXF-003).

Mobile is not affected: below 997 px the sidebar lives in the menu.
