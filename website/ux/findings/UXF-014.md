# UXF-014: the gate step runs about 30 minutes against 120 s

The step is one L2 test per page and gating viewport (278), each running axe in full (light) and
the colour rules (dark) plus the layout probes at three scroll states, with 2 workers. In R-01 the
gate run reached its 1,700 s limit after 184 L2 tests; two more runs measured the rest (76 tests in
1,699 s, 23 in 263 s). The host's load average was 90 to 176 throughout, so the times overstate
the cost, but even 8 s a test makes 278 x 8 / 2 = about 19 minutes.

`/ess/docs/reference/ess-ui` (665 KB of HTML, 757 table rows) ran past 60 s at both viewports in
two runs and is unmeasured in R-01: 548 of 556 configurations.

The organization's rule ("Tests stay fast") makes this a defect that gets a story before the next
feature. The fix is in the loop, not the site: axe once per page (both viewports from one load),
fewer probe states, or a gate over a sample of pages with the full sweep in the `slow` lane.

## R-02: closed

The gate lane (`UX_LANE=gate`, every merge) holds the 9 template pages of `adapter.ts` at both
gating viewports and both themes, the 2 anchor cards (pointer and keyboard) and the L1 tests: 54
tests. An L1 test fails when the template pages miss a page kind or an always-visible element of
the sitemap. The sitemap sweep, all 9 cards and the measured-only viewports run in the `slow`
lane, 8 projects, each one pool job with a 28-minute step limit.

`/ess/docs/reference/ess-ui` was slow in axe, not in the page: axe's default mode runs each frame
partly and aggregates the results in a new blank page, 92 to 112 s for one rule on its 17,337
elements; run in the page (legacy mode) the same rules took 16 to 30 s. The 3 s theme wait that
reloaded the page cost another 6 to 16 s on the long reference pages; after hydration it now waits
for the theme without reloading.

R-01's engine on the gate: cut at 120 s, "17 failed, 283 did not run, 8 passed", exit 1 after
134.6 s. R-02, three runs of the gate on engine 21e8c21e0346: 41.6, 29.1 and 28.7 s (6 workers, host
load 40 to 50; on engine 955825fcc512 at load 55 to 85: 65.6, 77.1 and 80.8 s); each failed only on site
findings, never on the budget.
