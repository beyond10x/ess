# UXF-032: the gate lane's scope in the baseline is stale

Change 1 of R-06 gave the page kinds `landing` and `doc-without-sidebar` to the slow lane. The gate
lane's scope in `baseline.json` still lists the 8 scenarios of the landing page and the visualise
page, and the round's decision names kinds and tests, not `gate: <scenario>` items, which the
ratchet needs to take a scenario a lane drops.

By the source, the next `task ux-gate` followed by `task ux-ratchet` (default round: the gate's
`round.json`) reports those 8 scenarios as missing values, and no L1 test holds `lanes.gate`
against `GATE_PAGES`. Not run: confirm with one `ux-ratchet` run on a gate round before the next
merge.

Affected: the next merge that runs the ratchet on a gate round. Cost S.

One line: move the 8 scenarios out of `lanes.gate` under a decision that names them
(`gate: <scenario>`), and hold the lane's list against `GATE_PAGES` with an L1 test.
