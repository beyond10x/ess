# UXF-012: the theme toggle's first press shows no change

The toggle cycles system, light, dark. A reader whose system theme is light sees the light theme
under "system"; the first press selects "light" and the page does not change. Switching to dark
takes 2 presses at desktop-1280 and 3 actions at mobile-390 (menu, then 2 presses); the task ends
at 3 and 4 actions against an expectation of 2.
