# UXF-005: graph nodes whose accessible name does not contain their visible label

axe `label-content-name-mismatch` (WCAG 2.5.3, serious): SVG groups of the domain graph
(`g[transform="translate(0,0)"]`, `g[transform="translate(360,0)"]` on the landing page) carry an
`aria-label` that does not contain the text they show. A speech-input user who says the visible
label does not reach the control.

2 pages, 4 configurations, 6 nodes: `/ess/` and `/ess/docs/visualise`, at both gating viewports.
Reported in the light theme only because the dark theme runs the colour rules alone
(`COLOUR_RULES` in `website/ux/core/contract.ts`).

## Owner (R-05)

The visualise page renders `<EssPresentation>` from `@beyond10x/ess-ui-templates`, pinned at `655a7e58bc66800c7b891515af2dc16c3a572782` in `website/package.json` (a commit of its branch `docs/serves-section`, pull request 4). Its `main` (`5b77e77`, tag 0.1.0) changed nothing under `web/` since that commit, so no newer commit of the package fixes the frame. The frame is the package's: this site does not copy or override its components, and the record waits on a fix there.

The landing page's domain graph is the shared docs theme's (`@beyond10x/docs-system`, `charts.js`, pinned at `5fd348a9` in `website/package.json`). Its commit `7fcf56d` ("a graph node's accessible name contains the text it shows", in its releases 0.15.0 and 0.15.1) fixes this rule there; moving the pin is a change of its own, not made in R-05.

## R-06: the landing page's graph is fixed by the shared theme's release

Change 5 of R-06 moved `@beyond10x/docs-system` in `website/package.json` from `5fd348a9` to
its release 0.15.0 (`81a87b17`), which holds `7fcf56d`. In the slow lane the two L2 tests of
`/ess/` passed where they were expected to fail, so they are no longer assets of this finding;
they stay in the slow lane as tests that pass. The visualise page keeps the finding: its frame is
the templates package's. The round's gate-5 counts are in its report.
