# UXF-004: links in running text differ from the text by colour only

axe `link-in-text-block` (WCAG 1.4.1, serious): a link inside a paragraph has no underline and too
little contrast against the surrounding text, so it is told apart by hue alone. The shot outlines
"Start here" in the first paragraph of `/ess/docs/concepts/adoption-modes`.

102 of 139 pages, 406 configurations, 2,702 nodes, in both themes and at both gating viewports.

`/ess/docs/concepts/ess`. The largest counts: `/ess/docs/releases/what-changed` (156 nodes) and
`/ess/docs/reference/spec-versions` (87).
