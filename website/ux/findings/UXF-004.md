# UXF-004: links in running text differ from the text by colour only

axe `link-in-text-block` (WCAG 1.4.1, serious): a link inside a paragraph has no underline and too
little contrast against the surrounding text, so it is told apart by hue alone. The shot outlines
"Start here" in the first paragraph of `/ess/docs/concepts/adoption-modes`.

102 of 139 pages, 406 configurations, 2,702 nodes, in both themes and at both gating viewports.
Examples: `/ess/docs/concepts/adoption-modes`, `/ess/docs/concepts/component-delivery`,
`/ess/docs/concepts/ess`. The largest counts: `/ess/docs/releases/what-changed` (156 nodes) and
`/ess/docs/reference/spec-versions` (87).

This class is 99 % of the round's axe nodes; one site-wide style rule likely covers all of it.

## R-03: closed by one style rule

The site had no stylesheet of its own. `website/src/css/custom.css`, registered as the classic
preset's `customCss`, underlines a link inside a paragraph, list item, table cell, definition or
quote of the page content (`.markdown`) in both themes; navigation, cards and heading anchors keep
their look. Before: the asset failed with `link-in-text-block` 1 node in each theme (a known
failure of this open finding). After: 0 in each theme, and the asset passes. Shots of
`/ess/docs/concepts/adoption-modes` at desktop-1280: `rounds/R-03/shots/link-underline-before-*`
and `link-underline-after-*`, light and dark. The whole site's count is in `rounds/R-03/report.md`.
