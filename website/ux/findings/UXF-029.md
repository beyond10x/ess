# UXF-029: the ess-ui reference page meets the 60 s test limit

`L2 /ess/docs/reference/ess-ui mobile-390` ran to Playwright's 60 s test limit in the slow lane of
R-06 (`slow-l2-6`, which started at a 1-minute load of 56.81, under the host's line of 60) and
passed in 14.4 s in the rerun `slow-rerun-a` (load 37.69). In R-05 the same page's desktop test
timed out in `slow-l2-5` (load 78.21) and passed in a rerun. A test that meets the limit at a load
under 60 is a test-budget defect by the host's rule (no test over 60 s), even though a rerun passes
it.

The page is one of the two long reference pages the gate gave up in R-05 (UXF-025): its L2 test
measures every gating configuration of a page of 17,841 built elements (the gate admits 1,200, `GATE_PAGE_ELEMENTS`) in one test; counted on the R-06 build.
