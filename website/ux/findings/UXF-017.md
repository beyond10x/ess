# UXF-017: navigation and table headers at about 11 px

Heuristic 15 asks for key data at 14 px or more. In the full-size shots at desktop-1280:

- the sidebar's top-level category names ("START HERE", "CONCEPTS", "REFERENCE" …) are uppercase,
  letter-spaced, with a cap height of about 8 px: about 11 px type;
- table column headers ("MODE", "COMMANDS", "WHAT YOU COMMIT" on
  `/ess/docs/concepts/adoption-modes`; "IDENTITY" in `worst-3-desktop-1280-dark.png`) are the
  same style;
- the table of contents is about 13 px (`uxf-010-desktop-1280-light.jpg`).

The sidebar categories are the only way into a section on a site without search (UXF-001), and the
column headers carry the meaning of every cell under them.

The theme's CSS (the docs-system package) is not in the tree, so the sizes are pixel estimates;
an L2 probe of the computed font size settles them.
