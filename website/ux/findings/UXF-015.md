# UXF-015: about 20 Tab presses to a sidebar link

The keyboard runs of the anchor cards all complete (4 of 4), but they cost far more actions than
the pointer runs of the same tasks:

| task | viewport | pointer | keyboard | expectation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| first-conformance-run | desktop-1280 | 2 | 23 | 3 |
| first-conformance-run | mobile-390 | 4 | 26 | 3 |
| install-linux-x86_64 | desktop-1280 | 2 | 21 | 4 |
| install-linux-x86_64 | mobile-390 | 4 | 24 | 4 |

From the top of a doc page, Tab passes the logo, the project switcher, seven navbar items and the
theme toggle (the shot) before the sidebar, then every open sidebar entry above the target. No
shortcut jumps to the sidebar or to a search field (the site has none, UXF-001).

One line: a keyboard shortcut to search or to the sidebar (with UXF-001's search) removes most of
the presses.
