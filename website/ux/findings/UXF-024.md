# UXF-024: a slice reports what it does not hold as unmeasured

Each slice of the slow lane (`l2-1` to `l2-6`) declares the lane's whole page count as its total,
so the reporter of one slice counts the pages of the other slices as undeclared and prints gate 1
and reduced motion as "fail" with one unmeasured unit. The merged round of the same runs passes
both gates with 0 unmeasured.

The merged round is right; a reader of a single slice's output is misled, and a slice that really
left a page unmeasured cannot be told from one that did not.

Affected: 6 of 11 runs of R-02 (`slow-l2-1` to `slow-l2-6`). Cost S.

One line: a run that holds a slice marks a gate "partial" for the pages other slices hold.
