# UXF-013: two extra taps per page link on a phone

At mobile-390 a link in the navbar or the sidebar is behind the menu button, and on a doc page the
menu opens on the docs panel, so a navbar link needs "Back to main menu" too. Tasks that take 2
actions at desktop take 4 at 390 px: `first-conformance-run` and `limitations` (expectation 3),
`install-linux-x86_64` (4); `page-a-view` takes 10 against 5 (the closed "Guides" category adds its
own taps).

The mobile-menu moment of the contact sheet shows the panel the menu opens on.
