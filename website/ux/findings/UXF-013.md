# UXF-013: two extra taps per page link on a phone

At mobile-390 a link in the navbar or the sidebar is behind the menu button, and on a doc page the
menu opens on the docs panel, so a navbar link needs "Back to main menu" too. Tasks that take 2
actions at desktop take 4 at 390 px: `first-conformance-run` and `limitations` (expectation 3),
`install-linux-x86_64` (4); `page-a-view` takes 10 against 5 (the closed "Guides" category adds its
own taps).

The mobile-menu moment of the contact sheet shows the panel the menu opens on.

## R-02: held to the card

Held to each card's limit (gate 4), the menu tap puts these phone runs over: `first-conformance-run`
4 of 3, `limitations` 4 of 3, `page-a-view` 11 of 5 (desktop-1280: 2, 2 and 4, within). The asset is
the anchor card `first-conformance-run` at mobile-390, which runs in the every-merge gate.

## R-04: owned by the theme

The panel the phone menu opens on is chosen by the theme, not by this site:

- `@docusaurus/theme-classic` 3.10.2, `lib/theme/Navbar/MobileSidebar/Layout/index.js`, renders the
  two panels and moves to the second (`navbar-sidebar__items--show-secondary`) when
  `secondaryMenuShown` is set;
- `@docusaurus/theme-common` 3.10.2, `lib/contexts/navbarSecondaryMenu/display.js`, sets it each
  time the menu opens on a page whose sidebar gives the second panel content (a doc page).

The shared theme, `@beyond10x/docs-system` at `5fd348a9e49a96832f70af7917e6cd2db2c32abe`, wraps
the classic theme and replaces neither component (its `dist/theme` holds `Navbar/Logo`,
`NavbarItem`, `DocItem/Content`, `Footer`, `TOCItems`, `Admonition`, `NotFound`). No option of
`docusaurus.config` names the first panel, and a rule of `website/src/css/custom.css` can only show
or hide a panel, not choose the one a tap opens: forcing the first would hide the docs panel and
its "Back to main menu" link. Changing it means a component of the theme, which this site does not
copy or override. The finding stays open, owned by the shared theme; R-04 took UXF-022 instead.
