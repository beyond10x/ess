# UXF-003: the release-post sidebar scrolls away, and is missing on the archive

The release posts under `/ess/releases` have a "recent posts" sidebar at desktop sizes, which the
adapter holds always visible. Scrolled to the end of a post or a list page, it is out of the
viewport: 68 pages, 133 configurations, 135 failures. Examples: `/ess/releases`,
`/ess/releases/a-cluster-described-and-answered`, `/ess/releases/a-deployment-described`.

`/ess/releases/archive` renders no sidebar at all (2 configurations).

Same mechanism as UXF-002 on the docs pages; kept apart because the blog layout is a different
component and may get a different fix.

## R-02: accepted under decision D15; the archive was the adapter's mistake

As for UXF-002, decision D15 (`rounds/R-02/decision.md`) gates the release sidebar at the top and
the middle of a page and reports the `end` state as measured only. The asset holds it on the
release post of the every-merge gate.

`/ess/releases/archive` renders no sidebar by design: "missing x2" came from the adapter, which
expected the blog sidebar on every `/ess/releases/*` page (review of R-01, T5). The adapter now
classes the archive as a release page without a sidebar (`blog-without-sidebar`).
