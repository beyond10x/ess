# UXF-019: "Get started" below the fold at 1280 x 800

At mobile-390 the landing page's first screen shows the primary action "Get started" and "See the
model". At desktop-1280 the hero text sits beside the tall code pair and starts about a third of
the way down; the first screen ends at the end of the lede, and neither action is in it. The only
next step in view is the navbar.

The evidence is the 330 px wide contact-sheet frame; a full-size shot of `/ess/` at 1280 x 800
confirms the exact position. No task card starts with "Get started" (the anchors go through the
navbar's "Docs"), so no round metric moves yet.
