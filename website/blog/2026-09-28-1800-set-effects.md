## Outcomes over many records

`instances: {where: …}` on a `moves:` or `updates:` changes every stored record the predicate
selects, and `{count: changed}` is how many it changed. `affects:` lets an outcome with one subject
change other records a filter selects, reading `subject.<field>`.

## Which outcome, and for whom

`unknown_instance:` beside an update makes a create-or-update, and `existing_instance:` with an
`error:` makes a create-or-refuse. An actor may declare `attributes:`, read as `{caller: …}` in
values and as `caller.<attribute>` in guards. `input_absent:` answers a request that carries no input at all.

## Retries, paging and values

A binding can bound its retry with `attempts` and `final` refusals. A view with `order_by:` can
declare `paging:`. A value may fall back to a literal after `else:`, or read a field of a related
record with `{related: …}`. `ess-composition/2` checks a consumer's types against the service it
imports.
